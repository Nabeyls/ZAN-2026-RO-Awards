03 · How to Nominate

Make your nomination easy to understand.

A strong submission should clearly show the value demonstrated, what the nominee did and the impact created.

01

Choose the Value

Select the Corporate Value that best reflects the nominee’s actions.

02

Tell the Story

A clear justification is required. Explain what happened and why the nominee deserves to be recognised.

03

Add Supporting Material

Supporting evidence is optional. You may include examples, photos, documents or a video to strengthen the nomination.

05 · Eligibility & Submission

Before the nomination is submitted

Check the nominee’s eligibility and prepare a clear justification before completing the nomination form.

Employment

Full-time ZUS employee

Nominations may be submitted for eligible full-time ZUS employees.

Service

More than 12 months with ZUS

The nominee must meet the minimum service requirement.

IR Record

Good disciplinary standing

The nominee must have no active or past Industrial Relations (IR) cases.

Justification

Required

Explain clearly how the nominee demonstrates the selected Corporate Value and the impact created.

Supporting Material

Optional

Examples, photos, documents, results or a supporting video may be included to strengthen the nomination.
