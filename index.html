ZAN RO AWARDS 2026

Best Zurista

Recognising outstanding Zuristas who consistently demonstrate strong performance, growth potential, initiative and positive contribution to their outlet team and customers.

⏰ Cut-off per guideline · 27 September 2026 · 11:59 PM

Nomination Criteria

✓Confirmed employee and not under probation.
✓Worked in the Zurista role for more than 6 months.
✓Zuristas currently in an OLIT position are nominated under this category.
✓Not under resignation notice, disciplinary record or ongoing IR case.
STEP 01 · NOMINATE

OM nomination

Only OM can nominate 1 or 2 full-time Zuristas under their care.

STEP 02 · FILTER

DM shortlist

Each DM filters 2 Best Zuristas from the nominated pool based on:

  • Potential Growth
  • Initiative & Strategic Thinking
  • Knowledge / Assessment Marks
  • Positive feedback from Team / Superior / Customer
STEP 03 · WINNER SELECTION

Final OPR review

Top average Zurista OPR marks (Jan–Aug 2026) from the finalist pool.

RO Committees pull out data for all nominees.

FAQ

Read me — it might answer your questions

FAQ · Bahasa Melayu

1. Bolehkah saya calonkan Zurista/OS/OM yang bukan dalam outlet jagaan saya?

Jawapan: Tidak boleh.

2. Bolehkah OM calonkan lebih daripada 2 orang untuk setiap kategori?

Jawapan: Tidak boleh. Pencalonan adalah minimum 1 nama atau maksimum 2 nama sahaja untuk setiap kategori.

3. Bolehkah saya beritahu calon bahawa saya telah mencalonkan dia?

Jawapan: Tiada masalah. Ini hanyalah pencalonan dan hanya pemenang yang terpilih akan menerima hadiah.

4. Saya OM, perlukah saya dapatkan approval DM sebelum membuat pencalonan?

Jawapan: Tidak perlu. OM boleh terus mencalonkan staff yang memenuhi kriteria. DM akan membuat semakan dan tapisan selepas pencalonan diterima. Pastikan pencalonan dibuat secara telus dan berwibawa.

5. Berapakah pemenang akhir untuk setiap kategori?

Jawapan: Jumlah pemenang dan nama pemenang hanya akan diumumkan pada malam ZAN, 21/10/2026.

FAQ · English

1. Can I nominate a Zurista/OS/OM who is not under my outlet?

Answer: No, you cannot.

2. Can an OM nominate more than 2 people for each category?

Answer: No. The nomination is a minimum of 1 name and a maximum of 2 names only for each category.

3. Can I inform the nominee that I have nominated them?

Answer: Yes. Make it clear that this is only a nomination and only the final winners will receive prizes.

4. As an OM, do I need DM approval before making a nomination?

Answer: No. OM can submit the nomination form provided the nominee meets the criteria. The DM will review and filter nominations afterwards. Nominations should be made fairly and with integrity.

5. How many final winners will there be for each category?

Answer: The total winners and their names will only be announced on ZAN Night, 21/10/2026.
